# Pitch

Five minutes per team, in front of at least two judges in Round 1. If we reach Round 2, we pitch again to a new panel with one representative from each prize category, and all participants vote for the Community Prize.

## What the organizers require

**The presentation covers:** the challenge, the user, the solution, how Open Payments was used, and key takeaways.

**The demo shows the payment flow:** payment request → authorization → payment execution → successful completion. We need it live and as a recording.

**Uploaded before Saturday 12:00:** slides and demo video, to the shared Google Drive folder the organizers send on the day.

## The four awards

Each award is US$5,000, every team is eligible for all four, and no team can win more than one ([key information](https://interledger.org/hackathon/2026-hackathon-medellin/)). The Round 2 panel includes a representative from each sponsor, so the pitch should speak to the one we're aiming for.

| Impact | Ruta N | How many people, how much money or time saved, and why Open Payments makes it possible |

## 5-minute structure

| Time | Section | Slide | What to say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:40 | Challenge | 1 | The problem, told through one person's story. One striking fact. |
| 0:40–1:00 | User | 2 | Who exactly has this problem and how they deal with it today. |
| 1:00–1:30 | Solution | 3 | What we built, in one sentence. Why it's better than today. |
| 1:30–3:30 | Live demo | — | The four moments of the payment flow (see the script below). |
| 3:30–4:15 | How we used Open Payments | 4 | The grants, payments and quotes behind what they just saw. One simple diagram. |
| 4:15–5:00 | Takeaways and impact | 5 | Who else this helps, what we'd build next, what we learned. Close with the team. |

The demo gets about 40% of the time. Judges remember what they saw working.

### Backup plan

Wifi fails, the Test Wallet is slow, a token expires. Assume something will go wrong.

- Record the demo video by Saturday 10:30 and keep it open in a tab, ready to play.
- Log in to the Test Wallet and open every tab you need before walking in.
- Use a fresh quote. Quotes expire, so don't create one 20 minutes before the pitch.
- If the live demo fails, don't debug on stage. Say "here's the recording" and play it.

## Slide outline

Five slides, plus a title slide. Little text, big visuals.

0. **Title:** project name, one-line description, team name.
1. **Challenge:** a person, a photo or quote, one number.
2. **User:** who they are, what they do today, what it costs them.
3. **Solution:** one sentence and one screenshot.
4. **Open Payments:** a diagram of the flow (wallet address → incoming payment → quote → grant → outgoing payment).
5. **Takeaways:** impact, next steps, team photo and names.

## Who presents

Pitching is a role, not an obligation. Choose what fits the team:

- **One presenter:** the person most comfortable on stage. Simplest and easiest to rehearse.
- **Presenter + driver:** one person talks while another runs the demo on the laptop. Good for someone who wants to be up front without speaking much.
- **Split:** one person tells the story (challenge, user, solution), another explains the technical part. Each speaks about what they know best.

Whoever doesn't present helps by rehearsing with the presenter, timing the run-throughs, and preparing answers to likely questions.
